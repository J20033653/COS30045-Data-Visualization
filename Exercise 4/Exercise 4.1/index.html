A House and Garden in SVG

Every part of this picture is an SVG shape written by hand in the page: rectangles, circles, ellipses, lines, polylines, a polygon, paths, text and groups.

The group element

Both windows sit inside one outer <g> that sets the fill, stroke colour and stroke width once, so they are styled identically. Each window is its own inner group whose shapes are drawn at (0, 0) and then moved into place with transform="translate(x, y)". Changing the outer group's fill recolours both windows at the same time.

What was customised

  • Moved and resized shapes to build the house, tree and pond.
  • Changed fill colours for the sky, grass, walls, roof and flowers.
  • Styled strokes: round-capped sun rays, white window frames and a thick white fence.
  • Added extras: a chimney with smoke, a pond, a fence, flowers, clouds and birds.
