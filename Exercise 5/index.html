TV Energy Consumption: Four Views

Four chart types built with D3, using the Australian television energy rating data. Each chart answers a different question about how much energy TVs use, and what electricity costs.

Energy consumption vs star rating

Each dot is one TV model. Higher star ratings should mean lower energy use, but screen size pulls the other way, so the trend is noisy.

TVs by screen size

How the TVs in the dataset split into small, medium and large screens. Medium-sized TVs are the biggest group, with large screens not far behind.

Average energy for 55-inch TVs

Comparing technologies fairly means comparing the same size, and 55 inches is the most common size in the dataset.

Electricity spot prices, 1998 to 2024

The average annual wholesale electricity price in Australia, which sets the running cost behind every kilowatt-hour a TV uses.
