Exploring TV Energy Use Interactively

Filter and explore the Australian television energy rating data. Choose a screen technology to see how its energy consumption is distributed.

How much energy do TVs use?

Each bar counts the TVs whose labelled energy consumption falls in a 100 kWh/year range. Most TVs use under 600 kWh a year, with a few power-hungry outliers stretching the chart to the right.

Does a higher star rating mean less energy?

Each dot is one TV. Colour shows the screen technology, and hovering over a dot shows its screen size, brand and energy use. Higher star ratings sit lower on the chart, but the spread within each rating is wide.
